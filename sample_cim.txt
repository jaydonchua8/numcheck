Company Overview

Harbor Fastener Supply ("Harbor" or the "Company") is a distributor of specialty
fasteners and assembly components serving 1,450 industrial customers from 6 branches
in the Midwest. The Company was founded in 1987 and employs 212 people. Management
estimates the addressable market at $3.2 billion.

In FY2024, revenue was $58.6 million and Adjusted EBITDA reached $9.4 million. Growth
has been driven by vendor managed inventory programs, which now represent 41% of sales.
Management expects FY2025 revenue of approximately $64.0 million.
