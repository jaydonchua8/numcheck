# Project Harbor: one-page summary (illustrative, written by hand for this repo)

## Business

Harbor Fastener Supply distributes specialty fasteners to 1,450 industrial customers from 6 branches. The Company was founded in 1987 and employs 220 people. Vendor managed inventory programs represent 41% of sales, and management sizes the addressable market at $3.2 billion.

## Financial snapshot

In FY2024, revenue was $58.6M and Adjusted EBITDA was $9.4M, a 16.0% margin. Revenue grew 13.0% in FY2024, up from $51.9M in the prior year. Gross profit reached $15.6M in FY2024, which is a gross margin of 31.0%. EBITDA of $9.4M in FY2024 compares with $6.8M in FY2023.

| Metric | FY2022 | FY2023 | FY2024 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $46.2M | $51.9M | $58.6M |
| Adj. EBITDA | $6.5M | $7.8M | $9.4M |
| Net income | $0.3M | $1.0M | $2.4M |
| Capex | $612 | $0.8M | $0.7M |

## Outlook

Management expects FY2025 revenue of $64.0M, which is growth of 9.1%. The revenue CAGR from FY2022 to FY2024 was 12.6%. At 8.5x Adjusted EBITDA the business would be valued near $80 million.
